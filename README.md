* 🔧 **Tech:** LLaMA 3 · LoRA · Hugging Face · PyTorch

* 📋 Generated structured clinical reasoning outputs

---

### 📚 RAG Document Intelligence System

> A knowledge-grounded AI system for document question answering and summarization.

* 🔧 **Tech:** LangChain · LLaMA · FAISS · Embeddings
* 📄 Processes and indexes domain-specific documents
* 🔎 Performs semantic retrieval over document chunks
* 💬 Generates context-aware answers and summaries
* 🎯 Designed to reduce unsupported LLM responses through retrieval grounding

---

### 🧠 MedSAM-2 — Brain Tumor Segmentation

> Deep-learning-based medical image segmentation for brain tumor analysis.

* 🔧 **Tech:** MedSAM-2 · Computer Vision · BRATS 2019
* 🧠 Evaluated automated tumor segmentation
* 📊 **Dice Score: 0.87**
* 🏥 Explored the application of foundation models to medical imaging

---
